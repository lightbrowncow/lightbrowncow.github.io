# 🐮 **LightBrownCow Project**

> _Guitar tuner._

---

## 🖋️ Introduction

Tired of ads in your guitar tuner? Me too! 
Why spend 5 dollars on purchasing some shit in the app store, when you can just code your own? 

That's why I wrote this simple guitar tuner pitch detection thing with a few criteria in mind: 

1. Free hosting
2. App-like (offline) experience

### Usage: 

1. Go to https://lightbrowncow.github.io/
2. Add it to homescreen.
3. Use it by enabling your phone's microphone (no, it will not spy on you).
